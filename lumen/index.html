The 90% figure echoes Andre, Boneva, Chopra and Falk (2024), Nature Climate Change: in a survey of nearly 130,000 people in 125 countries, 89% wanted their governments to do more on climate and 69% were willing to give 1% of their income, yet people systematically underestimated how willing their fellow citizens were to act. The spread model follows Mark Granovetter's threshold model of collective behaviour (1978). Lumen is a prototype by the Hope Library project.
